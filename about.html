EXECUTIVE HEAD CHEF

Marco Vellani

20 Years of Culinary Mastery

Born in Florence, Italy, Chef Marco Vellani's journey began in his grandmother's kitchen — a modest space that became the canvas for a legendary culinary career. Trained at the Institut Paul Bocuse in Lyon, France, Marco honed his craft under three Michelin-starred mentors before bringing his artistry to the stages of New York.

His philosophy is simple yet profound: "Every ingredient tells a story. My job is to let it speak beautifully." At DineCraft, every dish is a living testament to that belief — seasonal, sustainable, and deeply considered.

Marco's menus are driven by a relentless pursuit of balance — where bold flavors meet delicate technique, and where global inspirations are anchored by Italian culinary soul.

OUR CORE BELIEFS

The DineCraft Philosophy

Three principles guide everything we do — from sourcing to plating.

01

Seasonal Integrity

We build our menus around what nature offers at its peak. Each season brings a new canvas, and we paint with the freshest, most vibrant ingredients available from our trusted local farm partners.

02

Artisanal Precision

Every dish is a handcrafted creation — no shortcuts, no compromises. From hand-rolled pasta to slow-braised reductions, our kitchen operates with the discipline of a fine art studio.

03

Soulful Hospitality

We believe dining is an act of connection. From the first greeting to the final farewell, every interaction is crafted to make you feel genuinely welcomed, valued, and cared for.
